# Animal Crossing: New Horizons items Checklist

A simple web-based checklist for tracking your **Animal Crossing: New Horizons** collection.

The checklist currently includes:

* Fish
* Insects
* Sea Creasures
* Fossils
* Artwork
* Music
* Gyroids
* Reactions
* Villagers
* Special NPCs

## Live Website

**[Animal Crossing: New Horizons Checklist](https://ethanyifanju.github.io/ACNH_museum_checklist/)**

A simple, interactive checklist for tracking your Animal Crossing: New Horizons collection. Browse different categories, search for items, view detailed information, and mark items as collected. Your progress is saved locally in your browser.


## Features

* Track collected and uncollected items
* Search items by name
* Multi-language item names
* **Fish, insects, and sea creatures** include:

  * Appearance time and period
  * Sell price
  * Location
  * Shadow size and shape
  * Weather conditions
  * Moving speed
* **Art collection** includes:

  * Genuine and fake artwork
  * Artwork descriptions
  * Original artwork information
* **Music** includes:

  * Buy and sell prices
  * Obtaining methods
* **Gyroids** include different colour variations
* **Reactions** include obtaining methods and the type of villager who teaches them
* Progress tracking for each collection
* **Villagers** and **NPCs** include their basic information, such as their gender, hobby, personality, birthday and etc.
